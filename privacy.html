What this website does

Livin.Holic showcases products and links to online marketplaces where they can be bought. Visitors do not need an account, and payments are never taken on this website.

Information we collect

  • Contact messages. The contact form opens your own email app. We only receive what you choose to send us by email, such as your name, email address and message.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting and database providers may process basic technical data such as IP address and browser type to deliver the site and keep it secure.

How we use it

We use messages you send us only to reply to you. We do not sell your personal information.

Links to other websites

Product links take you to third-party marketplaces and online stores. Those sites have their own privacy practices and we are not responsible for them. Please read their policies before sharing personal or payment information.

Third-party services

This website uses Google Fonts for typography and Supabase to store and serve product information.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete any personal information you have emailed to us. Contact us using the details on the contact page.

Changes to this policy
