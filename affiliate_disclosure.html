Affiliate Disclosure

Some links on Livin.Holic may be affiliate links. This page explains what that means for you.

What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is a special link to a product on another website. If you click it and make a qualifying purchase, Livin.Holic may earn a small commission from the seller.

Does it cost you more?

No. The price you pay is set by the seller and is not increased because you used our link.

Our promise

  • We choose products to feature because we think visitors will like them, not only because of commission.
  • We do not sell products ourselves. Orders, payment, delivery and returns are handled by the seller.
  • Prices and availability can change on the seller's website at any time.

Questions

If you have any questions about how we link to products, please contact us.
