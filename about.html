Who we are

A home for lifestyle finds you will love

Livin.Holic is a place to discover fashion and clothing, jewellery, makeup and beauty, home decor and everyday lifestyle products. We bring them together in one easy-to-browse space.

When something catches your eye, the Shop Now button takes you to the online marketplace or seller where you can buy it.

Based in Tuala, Ngong, Kajiado County, Kenya.

How we work

We showcase

We collect and present products worth a look, with pictures, descriptions and prices where available.

Sellers deliver

We do not take payments, stock, ship or deliver. Payment, delivery and returns are handled by the seller.

You stay in control
